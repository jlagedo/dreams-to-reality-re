    About the game
    A dream world, seen from behind your hero
    
  
    
    
    
      
    Dreams to Reality is a third-person 3D adventure from Cryo Interactive, released in 1997 for Windows 95, DOS and 3dfx graphics cards. You guide Duncan, the "chosen one" a voice tells you that you are at the start, across floating dream islands: an overgrown stone temple in the clouds, an island of giant stone heads, a hammam, a cinema, a factory, a house of spiders, a shark arena and many more. The game ships about 95 distinct scenes.
    
      
    You run, jump, fly, swim and ride a surfboard or motorcycle. You fight with kicks and punches, a sword and a crystal bow, and you spend manna, your magical energy, on spells and abilities. Thirty items are tracked in the menu: fourteen spells and abilities (fire ball, healing, shield, invisibility, speed, resurrection and others) and sixteen objects, such as a key, letters, a record player and a lollipop. Characters talk to you in voiced, captioned dialogue, and what they say matters for the riddles.
    
      
    The game saves by itself each time you enter a new level. There is no manual save.

    Hints
    Tips for the demo
    
  
    
    
    Look after your manna
    Flying and magic cost manna. The small flying blue bubbles refill a lot of it. Watch the gauge, especially in flight: some places need more than others.
    
    
    Trust your shadow
    When you jump, the shadow you throw shows where you will land. The original game is unforgiving about falls.
    
    
    Read the dialogue
    Conversations hold the answers to the riddles. Picking fights with people who are not attacking you wastes life energy and can cost you information.
    
    
    Use magic on purpose
    The sword and the bow can become your standard weapons if you spend manna carefully. Some weapons suit close combat, others a distance.
    
    
    Explore everything
    Spells, bonuses and secret passages are hidden in the levels. Try the other camera presets with Alt+5 to 0 when the view is in the way.
    
    
    In the browser
    The game autosaves on each new level and your save stays in this browser. Click the game if the keys stop working. The original resets its options on every run.

    About this port
    Not an emulator: the original program, translated
    
  
    
    
    
      
    This is an unofficial fan preservation project. It has no connection with Cryo Interactive or any rights holder. It exists so that a 1997 game keeps being playable, and so that its code can be studied.
    
      
    The page runs the game's own Windows program, not a remake. The executable was statically recompiled: its x86 machine code was lifted ahead of time into C, then compiled to WebAssembly together with a small hand-written runtime that provides the Windows services the game expects (files, threads, windows, input, sound) on top of SDL3. There is no CPU emulator at run time.
    
      
    The picture is drawn by a WebGL 2 renderer written to reproduce the look of the 3dfx build (filtered textures, Gouraud shading) at the game's 4:3 frame, scaled to fit your window. Sound goes through Web Audio. Your autosave is kept in the browser's IndexedDB.
    
      
    What is different from the 1997 game
    
      
      
        
    • It is a demo. The data is a small cut of the game, downloaded once (the files and size are shown on the start screen) and cached by your browser. The full game is not here.
      • 
        
    • No CD. The original read its levels from the discs and played music from the CD's audio tracks. Here, only what is in the demo pack exists, so music may be missing.
      • 
        
    • No installation and nothing runs outside the browser tab. It needs a current desktop browser with WebGL 2 and cross-origin isolation (threads).
      • 
        
    • Window size and fullscreen are handled by the page, with a smooth or pixel-perfect scale. The original window sizes (F1 to F6) are not needed.
      • 
      
    
      
    Rights: the repository ships no game data. Whoever hosts this page is responsible for having the right to distribute the demo content it loads.

    Questions
    FAQ
    
  
    
    
    The keys do nothing, or the page scrolls.
    Click the game once: keys only go to it while it has focus. If the picture shows "Click to take control", click it. Press ? for the key list.
    
    
    Esc leaves fullscreen instead of opening the game's menu.
    Chrome and Edge let the game keep Esc in fullscreen. Other browsers reserve it: use the toolbar's Menu button, which sends Esc to the game.
    
    
    Is my progress saved?
    Yes. The game autosaves each time you enter a new level, and the page keeps that save in your browser's storage on this site. It is not uploaded anywhere. Clearing site data removes it.
    
    
    Why is there no music, or no voice?
    The demo contains only a slice of the game's data. The original played music from the CD's audio tracks, which are not part of the demo. Check that the speaker button is not muted and that the browser tab is not muted.
    
    
    Why is the picture 4:3 with black bars?
    The game renders at 640 × 480. The page keeps that shape instead of stretching it. Pixel-perfect in the toolbar uses whole-number sizes with sharp pixels; the default fills the space smoothly. Inside the picture, the game's own cinematic bars are part of the design.
    
    
    Which browsers work?
    A recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a desktop or laptop, with hardware acceleration on. It needs WebGL 2, WebAssembly threads (a cross-origin isolated page) and enough memory for the demo data. Phones and tablets without a keyboard cannot play it.
    
    
    It says the page is not cross-origin isolated.
    The game uses threads, which browsers allow only on pages served with two security headers. A correctly deployed copy of this page sends them. If you are running a copy yourself, recomp/web/package.py writes the headers file for Cloudflare Pages and serve.py serves it locally.
    
    
    The game stops or crashes.
    Reload; your autosave is kept. If it repeats, open the page with ?debug to see the log, and use "Copy technical details" in the error box when reporting it.
    
    
    Can I remove the downloaded data and my save?
      
    Your browser's site-data settings can do it, or use the button here. It deletes the cached demo data and the saved game, then reloads.
